The Problem

Every sink is a shortcut nobody should take.

Poured down a sink, cooking oil doesn't just disappear. It cools, solidifies inside the pipework, and builds up with everything else that gets rinsed away with it — one of the leading causes of blocked drains and sewer fatbergs.

Most properties already have a small brown caddy for food waste. Almost nobody uses it for oil — and that's actually correct: food waste caddies are meant for composting and anaerobic digestion, not liquid oil. Mixing the two contaminates a stream that has its own separate use, while oil has a use of its own too — as feedstock for biodiesel.

There's no proper answer for households right now. That's the gap Save Our Drains fills.

How It Works

A dedicated container. A proper collection round.

Your area signs up

A property owner, tenant, council or letting agent gets in touch — whether it's a single household or a whole street or estate.

We supply the container

A dedicated small container for used cooking oil only — separate from the food waste caddy, sized for kitchen use, free to participating properties.

We organise a periodic collection

Oil gets collected on a set local schedule, area by area, so it's picked up properly rather than piling up or ending up down the sink out of frustration.

It's recycled, not wasted

Same process as our commercial collections — routed to biodiesel producers and recovery partners instead of the sewer system.
