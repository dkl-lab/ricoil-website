What We Do

Four stages. One provider.

No handoffs between contractors, no gaps in the paperwork. RICOIL runs the full chain from your kitchen to the refinery.

01 / Collecting

Collecting

Scheduled or on-request pickups from restaurants, takeaways, care homes, pubs and food production sites. Where storage is hard to reach, we bring pumping equipment to extract oil directly — no awkward containers, no mess.

02 / Containing

Containing

Every collection leaves you with a washed, ready-to-use replacement container — no mess, no waiting, no odour left behind in your kitchen.

03 / Transporting

Transporting

Oil is moved under proper waste carrier documentation, with a transfer note issued for every job so your compliance record is always covered.

04 / Recycling

Recycling

Collected oil is routed to biodiesel producers and other recovery partners, turning kitchen waste into genuine renewable fuel feedstock and recovered byproducts.

Our Specialities

We don't do every waste stream. We do this one properly.

RICOIL specialises in used cooking oil — nothing else. That focus is what lets us solve the problems generalist waste companies don't bother with.

⛽

Pump-Assisted Extraction

Some storage set-ups just aren't accessible — buried behind equipment, awkward containers, tight kitchen space. Our teams carry pumping equipment to extract oil directly from wherever it's stored, so difficult access is never a reason a collection gets missed.

↺

Oil Exchange Programme

High-volume kitchens don't just dispose of oil — they buy it too. If your site works through a set quantity every few weeks, we can supply that fresh oil back to you at a competitive rate as part of your collection, turning your waste stream into a real saving on stock.

✓

Long-Term Partnerships

We'd rather earn a multi-year relationship than chase one-off jobs. Most of our accounts run on agreed contracts, giving you locked-in rates and a fixed schedule, and giving us the accountability to keep service prompt and consistent for the long run.

If it's tucked behind equipment or the container's awkward to shift, our pump does the work — nothing gets left uncollected because access is difficult.

About RICOIL

Built on the ground, not on a spreadsheet.

RICOIL started collecting waste cooking oil in 2011, well before the paperwork caught up with the business. Since then it's operated continuously — reorganised and re-registered along the way, but doing the same job it started with: taking used oil off your hands and putting it to proper use.
